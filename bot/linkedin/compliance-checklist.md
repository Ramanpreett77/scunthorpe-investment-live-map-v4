# SGJM Compliance Roadmap — UK Sourcing + Dubai Referrals

> General information, not legal advice. Confirm each item with a solicitor/accountant
> before trading. **Do not charge sourcing fees until items 1-5 are in place.**

## Phase 1 — Legal foundations (do before taking clients)

| # | Item | Where / notes |
|---|------|---------------|
| 1 | **HMRC AML registration** | gov.uk — register as an estate agent / property sourcer for anti-money-laundering supervision. Processing takes weeks; apply first. |
| 2 | **ICO registration** | ico.org.uk — £40/year data-protection fee. |
| 3 | **Redress scheme** | Join The Property Ombudsman (TPOS) or Property Redress Scheme. |
| 4 | **AML policies & risk assessment** | Written AML policy, customer due diligence (ID checks, source of funds) procedures. Templates exist via your redress scheme. |
| 5 | **Professional indemnity insurance** | Broker quote; often required by redress schemes. |
| 6 | **Client terms & fee disclosure** | Written terms of business; fees disclosed up front (Estate Agents Act 1979 obligations; no secret profits). |
| 7 | **Data protection docs** | Privacy policy (already on your website ✓), data retention policy. |

## What you CAN do while waiting for registration
- Build the audience: blog, LinkedIn carousels, networking ✅ (the automation does this)
- Educate investors; publish compliance-first content (builds trust) ✅
- Shadow experienced sourcers; view deals; build buyer lists ✅
- Sign referral agreements with Dubai partners (see below) ✅
- ❌ Do NOT charge a sourcing fee or sign clients as an agent until registered.

## Phase 2 — Dubai referral setup
1. Shortlist 3-5 **RERA-licensed** Dubai brokerages/developers with formal **introducer/referral programmes**.
2. Sign a written referral agreement: fee %, payment trigger (exchange/completion), exclusivity, branding rules.
3. Disclose your referral fee to every client you introduce (transparency = trust + compliance).
4. Keep your role to **introduction + general market education**; the Dubai partner handles advice, contracts and regulated activity in Dubai.
5. Account for the income (self-assessment); check whether any UK AML obligations attach to your side of the work with your accountant.

## Ongoing habits
- ID + source-of-funds checks on every investor client before introducing deals.
- Keep an audit trail: written terms signed, fees disclosed, due diligence notes per deal.
- Renew: ICO yearly, AML supervision yearly, PI insurance yearly.
- Never promise yields or "guaranteed" returns — education, not advice.
