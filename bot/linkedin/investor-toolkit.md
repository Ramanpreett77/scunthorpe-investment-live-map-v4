# LinkedIn Investor Finder — Toolkit

> ⚠️ **Compliance first:** LinkedIn does not allow scraping or automated people-search, and
> unofficial automation tools can get your account restricted. Everything below is a
> **100% compliant** approach: smart search strings + genuine outreach + a tracker.

---

## 1. Find investors with Boolean search

Paste these into LinkedIn's main search bar → **People** tab. Adjust the region to taste.

| Goal | Search string |
|---|---|
| General property investors near you | `"property investor" AND ("West Midlands" OR "Birmingham" OR "Solihull" OR "Coventry")` |
| HMO-focused investors | `("HMO" OR "house in multiple occupation") AND ("investor" OR "landlord" OR "portfolio")` |
| BRR / strategy-focused | `("BRR" OR "buy refurbish refinance" OR "flip") AND ("property" OR "investor")` |
| Company owners | `("Founder" OR "Director" OR "Managing Director") AND ("Property Investments" OR "Property Group" OR "Estates")` |
| Deal sourcers & agents | `("property sourcing" OR "deal sourcing") AND ("UK" OR "West Midlands")` |
| Finance partners | `("buy to let mortgage" OR "bridging finance") AND ("broker" OR "advisor")` |

**Pro filters** (use the "People" filter bar after searching):
- **Locations:** West Midlands, Birmingham, Solihull, Coventry, Wolverhampton
- **Industry:** Real Estate, Financial Services
- **Connections:** 2nd degree (warm-ish leads — you share a mutual connection)

💡 If you have **Sales Navigator**, filter by *Industry = Real Estate*, *Keywords = HMO/BRR*,
*Posted on LinkedIn in past 30 days* — active posters are the most likely to respond.

## 2. Groups to join (investors hang out here)

Search LinkedIn Groups for:
- **PINN — Property Investors Networking Group** (one of the largest UK communities)
- **UK Property Investors**
- **Birmingham Property Network / West Midlands Property Investors**
- **HMO Landlords UK**

Join 3–5 groups, then use *the group's own member search* — you can message fellow
group members more easily than strangers.

## 3. Hashtags to follow & post with

`#ukproperty` `#propertyinvestment` `#hmo` `#brr` `#buytolet` `#propertydevelopment`
`#westmidlandsproperty`

Follow each hashtag, engage with 5–10 posts per day, and include 3–5 relevant
hashtags on every post you publish (the auto-poster already does this).

## 4. Connection request templates (LinkedIn limit: 300 characters)

**Template A — fellow investor:**
> Hi {FirstName} — I run a property investment blog covering HMO and BRR strategies in the West Midlands. Always great to connect with fellow investors in the region and share what's working. Would love to connect.

**Template B — after engaging with their content:**
> Hi {FirstName}, your recent post about {topic} really resonated — I write about UK property investment (HMOs, BRR, refinancing) and I'm building my network of serious investors. Would be great to connect.

**Template C — sourcer / broker:**
> Hi {FirstName}, I invest in {strategy} around the West Midlands and I'm looking to build relationships with good local {sourcers/brokers}. Happy to connect and compare notes.

## 5. First message after they accept (day 1–2, no pitch)

> Thanks for connecting, {FirstName}! Quick question — what area or strategy are you
> focused on at the moment? I'm mostly around {region} doing {HMO/BRR}. Always keen to
> hear what's working for other investors locally.

Follow-up if they reply: share your latest article **only when it's genuinely relevant**
("I actually just wrote a breakdown on exactly this — happy to share if useful").

## 6. The 15-minute daily routine

1. **5 min** — engage meaningfully on 5 posts under the hashtags above (comment, don't just like).
2. **5 min** — send 5–10 personalised connection requests using the searches.
3. **5 min** — message new accepts, update `lead-tracker.csv`.

Consistency beats volume: ~50 targeted connections/week compounds fast, and every
article you auto-publish gives you a reason to start conversations.
