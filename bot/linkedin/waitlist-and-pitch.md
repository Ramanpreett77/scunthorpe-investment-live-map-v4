> Compliance line: registrations (HMRC AML, redress scheme) are triggered by doing
> *regulated* activity for fees. Everything below is **marketing / education /
> introductions only** — no sourcing fees, no negotiating for others, no client money.

budget range, strategy (HMO / BRR / BTL / Dubai), timeframe. Store in `lead-tracker.csv`
(Status column: "Waitlist").

>

**Waitlist DM (when someone comments):**

> targeting? I'll add you to the SGJM waitlist - first access when we launch, plus
> the free sourcing checklist right now.

## 2. B2B income NOW — sell marketing to the industry

You're a social media specialist. Property businesses are terrible at marketing.
Charge a retainer for *marketing services* (content, LinkedIn management, lead-gen
funnels) — that income needs **no** property registrations.

**Targets:** registered UK sourcers & buying agents, London/Midlands estate agencies,
Dubai brokerages marketing to UK buyers (search LinkedIn: "property sourcer",
"head of sales Dubai property").

**Connection request:**
> Hi {Name} - I'm a social media specialist specialising in UK property brands.
> I help sourcers and agencies fill their pipeline with LinkedIn content that
> actually converts. Would love to connect.

**Pitch DM:**
> Hi {Name} - quick one: I build LinkedIn/content systems for property businesses
> (weekly articles, carousels, investor lead funnels). Most sourcers I speak to
> have zero consistent content - and that's where their investors come from.
> I have a done-for-you package from £750/mo. Open to a 15-min chat this week?

**Why this is genius for you:** every client teaches you sourcing from the inside,
puts you inside compliant networks, AND funds your registrations + first deposit.

## 3. Dubai referrals — the compliant-now version

* Sign referral/introducer agreements with RERA-licensed partners now (signing is fine).
* Until registered & advised otherwise: **introduce, don't advise** - pass the contact,
  let the partner do everything regulated, disclose any fee to the client in writing.
* Content angle meanwhile: "UK vs Dubai" education (your blog already covers this) -
  education is always allowed.
