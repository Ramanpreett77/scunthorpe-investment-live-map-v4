Search any address in the top bar. This panel then builds one-click links to assemble its legal pack: title, EPC, planning, flood, HMO register + auction legal-pack search.
👆 Search an address to generate links…
HMLR can't be pre-filled (their portal is session-based + £3 paywall — no site can deep-link a specific title). 3 steps: 1️⃣ open Title Register above → Start now 2️⃣ paste the copied address into HMLR's search 3️⃣ pay £3 → download the register PDF.
Why links, not direct download? HM Land Registry titles (£3–£7) and auction legal packs sit behind paid / login-walled portals — no public API can legally auto-download them. These deep-links take you to the exact search page with the address/postcode pre-filled, so the pack is 2–3 clicks away.

No. Figures are illustrative assumptions only. They are not financial advice, a valuation, a guarantee or a promise of returns.

Use the alert form in the Deals area, complete the required fields and give consent. The alert endpoint must be configured before submissions can be received.
