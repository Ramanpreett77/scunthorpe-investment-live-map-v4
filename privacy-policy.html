Who I am

What data I collect

If you register for deal alerts, I collect the information you choose to submit:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • phone number, if supplied;
  • your preferred strategy;
  • your budget range; and
  • areas or postcodes of interest.

The form may also record the deal you asked about and the date and time of the registration. The website uses a technical anti-spam field and basic rate limiting. It does not intentionally collect special-category data.

Why I use it

I use the information to contact you about property sourcing opportunities that may match your stated preferences, respond to an enquiry, and keep a record of your consent. I do not sell your personal information or publish a list of alert subscribers.

Lawful basis

My lawful basis is consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting me using the details above. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Service providers

Google acts as a processor when the alert registration endpoint and Google Sheet are used to receive and store submissions. The private administration tool is a Google Apps Script web app restricted to the site owner. I will use appropriate account security and will not put a write-capable Sheet ID in this public website.

The public website also links to or requests information from services such as OpenStreetMap, postcodes.io, HM Land Registry, the UK House Price Index service and the EPC register. Those services may process technical information, such as an IP address, under their own privacy notices when your browser connects to them. External links open in a new service and are not controlled by this policy.

How long I keep it

I will delete alert-registration data after 24 months of inactivity, or sooner when it is no longer needed. I may keep a minimal record of a withdrawal or suppression request so that I do not contact you again where appropriate.

Your rights

Subject to legal limits, you can ask me to:

  • confirm whether I use your personal data and provide a copy;
  • correct wrong or incomplete information;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict or object to processing; or
  • provide your information in a portable format where that right applies.

To exercise a right, email [EMAIL]. I may need to verify your identity before acting. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. The optional analytics setting in config.js is disabled until the site owner enables a cookieless provider such as Plausible for the completed site domain. If enabled, it records privacy-friendly aggregate events such as deal views, alert-form submissions, WhatsApp-share clicks and deal-pack generation. It is not configured to send names, email addresses, phone numbers, exact addresses or deal notes.

The site uses browser session storage only for a short anti-repeat-submission check and browser storage for some on-device calculator/legal-pack features. The alert form does not intentionally keep your submitted personal details in browser storage. Deal share links use a public deal ID and are not investor-specific or tokenised; no investor identifier is put into a public URL. Third-party services and links may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you visit them; check their policies before using them.

Changes

I may update this policy when the service or the law changes. The date at the top will show when it was last updated.
