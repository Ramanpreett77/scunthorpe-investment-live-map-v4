💷 Deal Sourcing Fee Agreement

Template — complete the fields, print and sign.

1. Services

The Sourcer will identify, appraise and introduce investment property opportunities matching the Investor's stated criteria, and provide a deal pack for each introduced property.

2. Fee

Fees are stated of VAT where applicable.

3. Introduced deals

A property introduced to the Investor by the Sourcer remains a sourced deal. If the Investor buys it directly or via a connected party, the fee remains payable.

4. Figures and due diligence

Figures in deal packs (yield, GDV, refurb cost and rent) are estimates based on stated assumptions. The Investor must complete their own due diligence and take independent legal, tax and financial advice.

5. Compliance

Both parties will provide ID and source-of-funds information for anti-money-laundering checks. Personal data is processed under UK GDPR.

6. Cancellation and disputes

Governing law: England and Wales.
