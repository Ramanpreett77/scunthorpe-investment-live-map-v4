Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who I am. This blog is operated by the sole trader Ramanpreett Singh Chhabra, trading as SGJ. Contact: ramanpreettsinghchhabra@gmail.com.

What we collect. If you use the contact form or the chat assistant's lead capture, we receive the name, email address and message you choose to share. Newsletter sign-ups collect your email address and, if you give it, your name.

How we use it. Solely to respond to your enquiry or send the weekly deal digest and blog updates you requested. We never sell your data. Contact-form messages are delivered by our email form-delivery providers (Web3Forms and FormSubmit) on our behalf. Newsletter sign-ups join the same subscriber list as the main site's deal-alert form: your details are stored in a shared Google Sheet accessible only to me, and emails are sent via Google (Gmail with Google Apps Script). Every email includes an unsubscribe link that takes effect immediately.

AI assistance. Digest text is drafted with the assistance of an AI service (OpenAI) from public deal and blog data only. Your personal details are never sent to OpenAI and are never used to generate email content.

How long I keep it. I delete subscriber data after 24 months of inactivity, or sooner when it is no longer needed. To have your details deleted at any time, email me at the address above and I will remove your row from the subscriber list and confirm when it is done.

Analytics. I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect names, emails or phone numbers. It records aggregate page views and button clicks. I use Google Search Console to see which searches lead to this site.

Cookies & storage. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. When you leave details with the chat assistant they are passed to us (via our form provider) so we can respond, and a copy is kept in your own browser's local storage so nothing is lost if the page reloads.

Your rights. You may request a copy or deletion of any details you've sent us at any time by emailing ramanpreettsinghchhabra@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Third-party links. Articles link to external sites (e.g. social networks); their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.
